### The Problem
Deploying AI to remote cameras, robotics, and edge gateways is a nightmare:
1. **Flaky OTA Updates:** Multi-gigabyte container downloads fail over cellular IoT and brick remote hardware.
2. **Silent Model Drift:** Environmental shifts (glare, dust, lens degradation) cause undetected real-world accuracy loss.
3. **Expensive Truck Rolls:** A single technician dispatch to re-flash a bricked factory camera costs $1,500+.

### The Solution: MLOps.dev
An autonomous edge fleet control plane engineered specifically for physical silicon (Jetson Orin, RPi5, Coral, Intel NUC):
- **Delta Binary Patching:** Reduces 500MB neural network updates down to 12MB.
- **KL-Divergence Drift Engine:** Detects input tensor distribution shifts and trips autonomous rollback in $<300$ms.
- **Autonomous Canary Gates:** Phased hardware rollouts gated by live accuracy and latency budgets.
