# Offers lens
Score: 8/10 | Vote: FUND IF

## What is strong
1. **High-Pain, High-Budget Market:** Industrial automation and robotics companies lose $10,000+ per hour when an assembly line camera misclassifies defects or bricks during an update. These buyers have enterprise budgets and clear ROI.
2. **Clear Value Equation:**
   - **Dream Outcome:** Deploy updated vision models fleetwide with zero downtime and zero technician dispatches.
   - **Perceived Likelihood:** High because rollback is mathematical and automatic (instant 300ms recovery).
   - **Time Delay:** Near zero (instant OTA update).
   - **Effort & Sacrifice:** Low (one-line curl install on edge device).
3. **Price Disconnect:** At $149/mo for 50 devices ($3/device/mo) or $999/mo for enterprise, this is drastically cheaper than a single technician site visit ($1,500+).

## What would kill it (the top 3, most dangerous first)
1. **Lack of Proof & Trust:** Edge operators will never let an unproven agent manage firmware and models on million-dollar manufacturing lines without SOC2 compliance, verified security audits, and enterprise references.
2. **Weak Free-to-Paid Conversion Friction:** If the 10-device Starter tier is too generous, small robotics startups will stay on free tier forever without paying $149/mo.
3. **Commodity Creep:** If the platform is just seen as "Docker for IoT", balena or AWS Greengrass could clone the basic features.

## The questions I would ask the founder
1. What is the irresistible guarantee that eliminates all risk for a factory plant manager to install your curl command on their production Jetson?
2. How do you move a company from the 10-device free tier to the $149/mo tier within 30 days?
3. Could you charge $499/mo instead of $149/mo by packaging a "zero-brick warranty" and automated compliance reporting?

## If FUND IF: the conditions
1. Create a "Zero-Downtime SLA Guarantee": If an OTA update fails or causes model degradation, the platform guarantees automatic rollback in under 1 second or the entire year is free.
2. Gate critical enterprise features (automated canary gates, SLA drift alerts, team RBAC, audit log export) behind the paid tiers so 10-device teams convert as soon as they deploy commercially.
3. Package an onboarding concierge: Offer a free 30-minute silicon hardware audit and migration of their first ONNX/TensorRT model.
