The problem

Factory floors break CI/CD pipelines.

Industrial environments have intermittent connectivity, metal-shielded Wi-Fi dead zones, and 24/7 uptime requirements. Standard cloud-first MLOps tools assume reliable network access. They fail silently when they do not have it.

PROBLEM 01
Silent model staleness
Devices lose connectivity during shift changes. Models go stale. No alert fires. The defect detection model from 3 weeks ago is still running — you find out when a quality audit fails.
PROBLEM 02
Heterogeneous hardware
Your floor has Jetson Nanos, custom ARM PLCs, and legacy x86 industrial PCs. A single model format and a single deployment script cannot handle all three.
PROBLEM 03
No rollback under pressure
A bad model update triggers false positives on the production line at 2am. There is no rollback button. An engineer SSHes into 40 cameras one by one.
How MLOps.dev solves it

Built for manufacturing AI.

Drift detection on-device
KL divergence checks run locally every 100 inferences. Lighting changes, product line switches, and camera fouling are caught before your operators notice.
Offline-first sync
6-hour outage tested on 2G. Telemetry buffers locally. Model updates queue. On reconnect, everything reconciles — chronologically, idempotently, without duplicates.
Hardware-class canary
Roll to Jetson Orins first, validate, then expand to Nanos. Each hardware class gets the right model format. One rollout, health-gated.
Immutable audit logs
Every deployment, every drift event, every rollback — timestamped and immutable. Required for ISO 9001 quality audits and traceability reports.
Fleet visibility
Live dashboard shows every device: model version, drift status, latency, last-seen. Not a spreadsheet — a real-time view of your production floor.
One-click rollback
Bad update on the line? Roll back the entire fleet or a specific device group in under 90 seconds from the dashboard.
We were managing 800 factory cameras with Bash scripts and a spreadsheet. MLOps.dev replaced that in one afternoon. The offline sync works through our factory floor Wi-Fi dead zones.
